### uk-dpa-age (answer) -- WARN
Q: Under the Data Protection Act 2018, what is the minimum age at which a child can consent to information society services in the UK?

A: The minimum age at which a child can consent to information society services in the UK under the Data Protection Act 2018 is 13 years old. ⚖️

- why: Section 9 of the Data Protection Act 2018 explicitly modifies Article 8(1) of the UK GDPR so that references to '16 years' are read as '13 years'. The ICO's guidance on children's data states that only children aged 13 or over may give their own consent for an information society service when consent is the lawful basis.
